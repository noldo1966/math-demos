Answer Every Question

A Proof Standard for Mathematics Produced with AI. Arnoldo Miranda.

The paper proposes four requirements for whoever presents a mathematical result, however it was produced:

  1. State what you assume.
  2. Prove everything above it.
  3. Answer every question, at any depth, whatever its tone, critique or validity, from your own understanding.
  4. Keep answering for as long as the result is in use.

These are the four requirements as the paper's Conclusion states them (Section 10). Section 4.1 gives them in full.
