The red area in this square puzzle has no single answer

The puzzle gives a 10 cm square, a bent line made of a 4 cm piece and a 6 cm piece meeting at a right angle, and asks for the red area. Those facts fix the 4-6 right triangle completely, but not how far it is tilted. Drag the slider: every position below satisfies every condition in the puzzle, and the red area changes.

Why: the proof, step by step

Put the origin at the bottom-left corner of the square, 1 unit = 1 cm. Call the top-left corner D. Call A the point where the 4 cm piece meets the left side, B the bend, and C the point where the 6 cm piece meets the top side. Let θ be the angle the 4 cm piece makes below the horizontal, c = DC (along the top), and d = DA (down the left side).

  1. The white region is two right triangles. The segment AC splits it into triangle ABC (right angle at B, given) and triangle ADC (right angle at the corner D). So
    Red = 100 − area(ABC) − area(ADC) = 100 − ½·4·6 − ½·c·d = 88 − ½·c·d
  2. Place B. Going 4 cm from A = (0, 10 − d) in direction (cos θ, −sin θ):
    B = (4 cos θ, 10 − d − 4 sin θ)
  3. Place C. The 6 cm piece is perpendicular to AB, heading up, in direction (sin θ, cos θ):
    C = (4 cos θ + 6 sin θ, 10 − d − 4 sin θ + 6 cos θ)
  4. C must be on the top side (its height is 10). Setting the second coordinate equal to 10:
    d = 6 cos θ − 4 sin θ,   c = 4 cos θ + 6 sin θ
    Check: c² + d² = (16cos²θ + 48 sinθ cosθ + 36 sin²θ) + (36cos²θ − 48 sinθ cosθ + 16 sin²θ) = 52 = AC², as the Pythagorean theorem requires.
  5. Multiply.
    c·d = 24cos²θ − 16 sinθ cosθ + 36 sinθ cosθ − 24 sin²θ = 24 cos 2θ + 10 sin 2θ
    using cos²θ − sin²θ = cos 2θ and 2 sinθ cosθ = sin 2θ.
  6. The red area as a function of the tilt. Since √(12² + 5²) = 13, write 12 cos 2θ + 5 sin 2θ = 13 cos(2θ − φ) with φ = arctan(5/12) ≈ 22.62°. Then
    Red = 88 − ½·c·d = 88 − 12 cos 2θ − 5 sin 2θ = 88 − 13 cos(2θ − φ)
  7. Every tilt in range is a real figure. We need c > 0 and d > 0, which means −33.69° < θ < 56.31° (tan θ between −2/3 and 3/2). The bend is always inside the square because its height is 10 − 6 cos θ, between 0 and 10, and its distance from the left side is 4 cos θ, between 0 and 10. The square is convex, so both pieces stay inside it.
  8. Conclusion. The red area depends on θ, which the puzzle does not give. Its smallest value is 88 − 13 = 75, at 2θ = φ (tan θ = 1/5, θ ≈ 11.31°, where c = d = √26). As θ approaches either end of its range, the corner triangle shrinks to nothing and the red area approaches 88 without reaching it. So the red area can be anything from 75 up to, but not including, 88 square centimeters.
